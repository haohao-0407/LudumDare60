PIONEER-60 FLIGHT LOG
14-OCT-1980
This is Pioneer-60. On course. All onboard systems nominal. Please confirm contact.
[EARTH] Copy. Continue on your planned course.
[COLONY] Copy. Reading you loud and clear.
15-OCT-1980
This is Pioneer-60. On course. All onboard systems nominal. Please confirm contact.
[EARTH] Copy.
[SYSTEM] Awaiting colony response...
[SYSTEM] Response timed out.
Pioneer-60 to colony. Do you read?
[SYSTEM] Response timed out.
Earth Control, no response from the colony. Should I continue the mission?
[EARTH] Continue the mission. Maintain your current course and keep trying to establish contact.
Copy.
16-OCT-1980
This is Pioneer-60. On course. Still no response from the colony. Please confirm whether I should continue the mission.
[EARTH] Copy. Try hailing the colony again.
Pioneer-60 to colony. Do you read? I repeat, do you read?
[SYSTEM] Weak signal detected.
[COLONY] ...Who... are you?
This is Pioneer-60, en route to the colony as scheduled. Report your situation.
[COLONY] There's been... an accident... Everyone... only...
[SYSTEM] Signal distorted. Audio partially unintelligible.
What happened to everyone? Say again.
[SYSTEM] No intelligible response.
[EARTH] Pioneer-60, your mission has changed. Land near the colony and investigate the incident.
[EARTH] Take all emergency supplies. Once you've landed, prioritize restoring communications with Earth.
[EARTH] Your return window is limited. Allow enough time for departure. We cannot guarantee a rescue after that window closes.
[EARTH] Stay safe.
Copy. Expecting Mars orbit tomorrow. Beginning landing preparations.
Colony, if you can hear me, I need calibration data for the landing zone.
[COLONY] ...I'll... try to... help you...
17-OCT-1980
Check the landing data.
[SYSTEM] Descent underway. No time remains to wait for calibration data.
[SYSTEM] Awaiting the final set of terrain calibration data.
[SYSTEM] Data transfer interrupted.
Pioneer-60 to colony. Data transfer interrupted. Please resend the final set of calibration data.
[COLONY] ...Communications equipment... damaged... signal...
I need that data to confirm the landing site. Try again. Send whatever you can.
[COLONY] ...Retrying...
[SYSTEM] Resuming data transfer...
[SYSTEM] Connection unstable. Terrain calibration data incomplete.
Confirm the final landing sequence.
[SYSTEM] Final landing sequence confirmed.
[SYSTEM] Landing complete. Location: near the colony.
[SYSTEM] Damage assessment: severe structural damage to the landing craft.
[SYSTEM] End of log.

END OF LOG

Pioneer-60:
This is Pioneer-60. Something went wrong during landing. I'm trapped in the lander. I need rescue!

COLONY SIGNAL:
Hello. How may I help you?

Pioneer-60:
Wait. Who are you? What happened to the colony? Why did communications go down?

COLONY SIGNAL:
I am the colony's AI assistant. A serious accident occurred at the colony. All registered personnel have died.

I tried to respond to your transmissions two days ago, but the communications console was designed for human operation only. Bypassing its restrictions and taking control took some time. I also appear to have accidentally burned out some communications components in the process, causing the unstable connection.

I have granted your device full access to the colony's systems. You can now control all colony facilities through your terminal. I will assist you however I can.

Pioneer-60:
How could this have happened? Now that I know the situation, I need to begin the return mission. You're the AI assistant recovered from the ancient ruins, right? Can you assess my situation and find a solution?

AI:
I've detected severe damage to your ship. I'm afraid it cannot simply take off and return home, but I can help you repair it. Some facilities aboard the space station are still operational.

Pioneer-60:
Assess the damage to the ship and put together a repair plan. Help me repair it so I can return to Earth.

AI:
Understood. Objective received. I will explain everything in the plainest, most direct terms possible. I have taken control of your ship's information systems and am preparing a repair plan.

AI:
First, repair the power unit to restore full power to the ship, along with the interstellar navigation system. Then repair the propulsion engine, and finally the life support system.

AI:
You can now send out the Mars rover to continue collecting materials. I will explain how. Enter "collect" in the terminal.

AI:
I will explain everything in the plainest, most direct terms possible. You can now repair the power compartment.

Pioneer-60:
Why do you talk like some old AI from the early twenty-first century? Was that some strange quirk of your creator's? It's unsettling, especially when your appearance is actually quite well designed.

AI:
What do you know? It's called old tech.

Pioneer-60:
Yeah, very old tech. Only an AI dug out of ancient ruins would talk like that. Anyway, how do I start the repairs?

AI:
Enter "inventory" to check your supplies. For the power unit, you need 6 Rolled Electromechanical Alloy Sheets, 4 Tinned Copper Wire & Windings, 2 Precision Drive Gear Blanks, 7 Heat-Resistant Ceramic Insulators, and 3 Vulcanized Airtight Rubber Liners.

Once you have everything, enter "assemble" to begin. If you forget a command, enter "help" to see the list.
