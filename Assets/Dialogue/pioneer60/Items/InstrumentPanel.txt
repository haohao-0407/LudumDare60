Discarded Electromechanical Instrument Panel

Description:
A bank of brass-rimmed analog gauges beneath a curved, dust-protective glass cover coated with space dust. Slender needles sit frozen against faded scales, with levers and tiny adjustment knobs protruding from the side. Aboard the colony ships of the old era, it served as the senses of an entire compartment, providing readings of pressure, energy flux, and temperature.
