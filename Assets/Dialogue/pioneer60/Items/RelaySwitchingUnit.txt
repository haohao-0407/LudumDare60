Electromechanical Relay Switching Unit

Description:
A boxy metal housing covered in scorch marks, densely packed with electromagnetic relays, copper terminals, and stepping dials. This was an ancient civilization's primitive logic center, opening and closing contacts to transmit signals and coordinate the compartment's electromechanical equipment. It could perform complex timing control without silicon chips. Remarkable.
